01Beginning
展開

It all began with a cup of coffee.
一切，都由一杯咖啡開始。

In 2010, in Saint Petersburg, I had a cup of Danesi Caffe.
2010 年，我喺聖彼得堡飲咗一杯 Danesi Caffe。

The taste of that coffee stayed with me.
嗰杯咖啡嘅味道，一直留喺我心入面。

Later, I went back to Hong Kong and opened my own café, mymy Caffe.
後來，我返到香港，開咗自己嘅咖啡店——mymy Caffe。

I also travelled to Tibet, India, Siberia, and Nepal.
我亦去過西藏、印度、西伯利亞同尼泊爾。

But Russia was different.
但俄羅斯，對我嚟講有啲唔同。

I went there twice.
俄羅斯我去過兩次。

The second time, in 2025, I chose to experience life on the Trans-Siberian Railway.
第二次係 2025 年，我選擇體驗西伯利亞火車生活。

Along the way, I read a book about Lake Baikal and was drawn to its beauty.
沿途，我讀咗一本關於貝加爾湖嘅書，就迷上咗她嘅壯麗。

I started to collect information about Lake Baikal, prepare my clothes, and plan the trip.
我開始搜集貝加爾湖嘅資料、準備衣物，同埋計劃行程。

Finally, I arrived at Lake Baikal.
終於，我嚟到貝加爾湖。

02No Currency Exchange in Irkutsk?
不存在嘅找換店

Of all the things on this trip, exchanging money turned out to be the most unforgettable—and the most frustrating.
今次旅程最難忘、最挫敗嘅，竟然係唱錢。

It was my third trip to Russia, yet this time I nearly ran out of money to spend.
竟然第三次去俄羅斯先失手，差啲冇錢使。

I had US dollars with me, but only a small amount of roubles left over from an earlier trip.
我身上帶咗美元，但盧布就只得之前旅程用淨嘅少少。

On previous trips, I had found currency exchange shops at DME Airport and in Vladivostok.
之前喺 DME 機場同海參崴，我都搵到找換店。

In Irkutsk, though, I had to go to a bank.
但喺伊爾庫茨克，我就要去銀行唱錢。

The banks were closed on Saturday afternoon.
銀行星期六下晝休息。

There was a limit on how much I could exchange in a single transaction, ranging from USD400 to USD600.
每筆交易都有兌換限額，由400至600美元不等。

The banks would not let me exchange money a second time on the same day.
銀行唔接受我喺同一日再唱第二次錢。

I went to two banks, but still could not exchange enough for the trip.
我跑咗兩間銀行，但都係唱唔夠旅程所需嘅盧布。

The bank staff did not speak English, which made things even harder.
銀行職員唔講英文，溝通就更加困難。

With only ten or twenty thousand roubles, I simply did not have enough for the trip.
手上得一萬幾千盧布，真係唔夠成個旅程用。

Luckily, the guesthouse owner was willing to help me sort it out. Otherwise, I would have ended up sleeping on the street.
好彩，民宿老闆肯幫我解決，如果唔係我就要瞓街。

Guesthouses around Lake Baikal generally collect payment on arrival.
貝加爾湖周邊嘅民宿一般係到付收費。

03Irkutsk: The Gateway to Khuzhir
伊爾庫茨克係進島，必經之路

Before heading to the island, I stopped in Irkutsk.
進島之前，我先去伊爾庫茨克。

I exchanged money and bought bus tickets, I enjoyed the food.
要換錢，要買巴士車票，要享受美食。

I could not miss a performance at the theatre.
絕對唔可以錯過係劇院嘅任何一場精彩表演。

And there was the Angara River.
還有安加拉河。

Food is my favourite part of travelling in Russia.
俄國美食，係我遊俄羅斯最喜歡嘅部分。

Mors, pasta, bread, desserts, and coffee.
Mors、義粉、麵包、甜品，還有咖啡。

Everything was skillfully made and delicious.
全部都巧手製作，非常美味。

After dinner, I went to the theatre to enjoy an opera.
吃飽後，晚上就去劇院欣賞歌劇。

04How to Live at -30°C
想像唔到零下30度嘅生活

Only when I felt fully prepared did I dare to set off.
我做足準備，先敢出發。

From my experience travelling alone, I knew that the information I could find online was limited.
以我獨旅嘅經驗，我知道網上搵到嘅資訊其實好有限。

In the end, I chose two guesthouses on Olkhon Island. Both had saunas.
最後，我選咗奧利洪島兩家民宿都有桑拿。

Life at -30°C was far from easy.
負30度嘅生活，真係唔簡單。

I set off from the guesthouse on my own and decided to walk instead of taking a taxi.
我一個人由民宿出發，決定唔搭的士，試下行路。

I ended up walking several kilometres.
結果，一行就行咗幾公里。

Walking through the snow was not easy. Each kilometre took me twice as long as usual.
喺雪地上行走，唔係一件容易嘅事。每行一公里，都要用平時兩倍嘅時間。

I found a supermarket and bought some supplies: milk, yoghurt, cake, cup noodles, and sparkling water.
我搵到超市，買咗補給品：牛奶、酸奶、蛋糕、杯麵，仲有氣泡水。

On the way back, I continued through the wind and snow.
回程，我繼續頂住風雪前行。

The road through Khuzhir, on Olkhon Island, was completely covered in snow.
奧利洪島上，胡日爾嘅公路完全被白雪覆蓋。

The freezing wind was so strong that I could barely move forward.
冰冷嘅風好強，吹到我幾乎行唔郁。

It reminded me of Tibet, at 4,500 metres above sea level, and the Annapurna Base Camp trail in Nepal, at 4,300 metres.
我諗起海拔4,500米嘅西藏，同埋尼泊爾海拔4,300米嘅安納普爾納大本營步道。

My body seemed to slow down on its own.
我嘅身體，好似自然就慢咗落嚟。

Each step felt less like a struggle and more like something to treasure.
每一步，感覺唔再只係艱難，而係值得珍惜。

I could hear the wind and my own breathing.
我聽到風聲，亦聽到自己嘅呼吸聲。

It was freezing, but I felt very awake.
雖然好凍，但我覺得自己好清醒。

I felt the power of nature, how vast the world was, and how small I was.
我感受到大自然嘅力量，世界有幾遼闊，而自己又係幾咁渺小。

For a moment, it felt like I was the only person in the world.
有一刻，感覺成個世界好似只剩低我一個人。

05North or South
南線北線

In the end, both the south and north routes of Lake Baikal were equally wonderful.
最後，貝加爾湖嘅南線、北線，一樣精彩。

Lunch by the lake, walks along the shore, and now and then, a deep rumbling sound.
湖中午餐、散步，間中傳來隆隆聲巨響。

I think that was the lake breathing.
我覺得，應該係湖水嘅呼吸吧。

The lake and the ice became one.
湖與冰，融為一體。

In the distance, I saw people hiking.
遠處，我見有人行山。

At sunset, I saw dogs walking up the mountain alone, with no owners.
日落時，我見到狗狗獨自走上山睇日落，無主人陪伴。

The frozen lake at sunset was incredibly beautiful.
日落時嘅冰湖，美得令人難以置信。

I could hear the wind blowing through the snow.
我聽到風吹過雪地嘅聲音。

I could see my own footprints in the snow.
我見到雪上自己嘅腳印。

In Khuzhir, there is Russian massage with Andrey Dikaev, and a sauna too.
I love them both.
胡日爾村有俄式按摩(Andrey Dikaev)，又有桑拿。
兩樣都係我至愛。

I also went to the post office to send a postcard, it would take five weeks to arrive safely.
我搵到郵局寄咗兩張明信片，要五個星期先安全到手。

There were no overly commercial shops here.
呢度唔會有過度商業化嘅店舖。

I could feel that people were living freely and comfortably.
我感受到呢度嘅人，自由自在地生活。

At Lake Baikal, you can glide across the frozen lake,
feel the cold, and find the right balance and rhythm.
嚟到貝加爾湖，可以係冰湖上滑行。
體驗冰冷嘅生活，再找到合適嘅平衡同節奏。

Life, or skating, it is all the same.
生活也好，溜冰也好，都一樣。

06aThe Lake Breathes
用心感受冰湖嘅呼吸

The lake was very quiet, but the surface was not flat.
湖面很安靜，但並不平坦。

Ice pushes against ice, until it bursts open, layers of ice are buried on top of each other.
冰與冰互相撞擊，直到爆裂一刻，冰層就會層層疊埋。

And between the cracks, white drifting snow hides.
裂紋之間，會藏著白色嘅漂雪。

There was drifting snow on the ice, and the wind was blowing it away.
冰上有漂雪，被風吹散。

I lay down and pressed my ear against the lake, I could hear the lake breathing.
我躺下，耳仔貼近湖面，我聽到湖在呼吸。

It was a deep, slow sound, with bubbles underneath.
係一種深沉而緩慢嘅聲音，底下還有氣泡。

I felt very calm.
我感到非常平靜。

It felt like I was connected to the lake.
感覺自己與湖連結一起。

06bSunday in Listvyanka
星期日嘅利斯特維揚卡

From the window, I saw many people playing on the frozen lake, it looked like a scene from a movie.
我望住窗外湖面，見到好多人係冰湖上玩樂，好似電影嘅畫面。

I could hear the wind, and I could hear laughter from far away.
我聽到風聲，也聽到遠處嘅歡笑聲。

I felt surprised.
我感到驚訝。

It felt like the world had suddenly become quiet, but it was still full of life.
世界彷彿突然安靜下來，卻依然充滿生命力。

07Returning
歸途

On the way back, I kept thinking about the lake.
歸途中，我一直諗起貝加爾湖。

I thought about the snow, the wind, the frozen surface, and the sound of the lake breathing.
白雪、寒風、結冰嘅湖面，還有湖嘅呼吸聲。

I realised that this trip was not just about seeing a place.
我開始明白，呢次旅程唔單子係去咗一個地方。

It was about feeling life in a different way.
而係用另一種方式，感受生命。

At Lake Baikal, I learned to slow down.
係貝加爾湖，我學識咗放慢腳步。

I learned to listen.
學識咗聆聽。

I learned to be present.
學識咗活在當下。

The lake taught me that life can be quiet, but still full of power.
貝加爾湖讓我明白，生命可以很安靜，卻依然充滿力量。

08Ending
尾聲

When I returned, I brought this feeling back with me.
回來後，呢份感覺沉澱咗之後。

It became part of my life map.
它成為我人生地圖嘅一部分。

It became part of my stories.
也成為我故事嘅一部分。

And slowly, it became part of my universe.
慢慢地，它也成為我宇宙嘅一部分。

From a cup of coffee to a universe, this is how my journey continues.
從一杯咖啡到一個宇宙，我嘅旅程就係咁樣延續落去。
